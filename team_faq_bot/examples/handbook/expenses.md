# Expenses

## What you can claim
Travel for client visits and conferences, a home-office setup budget of $500 in your first
year, and one team meal per month (up to $40 per person). Personal travel and alcohol on
your own aren't covered.

## How to claim
Submit claims in Expensify within 30 days, with a photo of each receipt. Claims under $75
need only your manager's approval; anything above also needs Finance (finance@example.com).
Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Travel booking
Book flights and hotels through TravelDesk so they're paid directly. Economy class for
flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.
