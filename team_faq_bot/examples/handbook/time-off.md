# Time off

## Holiday allowance
Everyone gets 25 days of paid holiday a year, plus public holidays. Allowance is
pro-rated for part-time staff and for people who join or leave mid-year.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be used by 31 March.

## Booking holiday
Book time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than
3 days. Your manager approves it. Add it to the team calendar once approved.
During the December code freeze (15–31 December) at most half the team can be off at once.

## Sick leave
If you're ill, message your manager before 10am and post in #team-status. You don't need
a doctor's note for up to 5 working days. Longer absences need a note and a quick chat with HR.

## Parental leave
Primary carers get 20 weeks at full pay, and secondary carers get 6 weeks at full pay,
after 6 months with the company. Talk to HR at least 15 weeks before the due date.
