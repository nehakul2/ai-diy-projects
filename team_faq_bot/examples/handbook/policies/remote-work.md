# Remote and hybrid work

## Office days
We're hybrid: the team meets in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Other days you can
work from home or anywhere in your home country.

## Working abroad
You can work from another country for up to 4 weeks a year. Tell your manager and HR at
least 2 weeks before, because of tax and insurance rules.

## Equipment
You get a laptop and can keep the monitor and chair from your home-office budget (see
Expenses). Report lost or stolen equipment to IT (#it-help) within 24 hours.

## Core hours
Be available between 10am and 3pm your local time for meetings. Outside that, work the
hours that suit you.
