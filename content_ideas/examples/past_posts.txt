POST 1
I shipped a feature last month that nobody used.
Not because it was bad. Because I never asked the 3 customers who requested it how they'd actually use it.
Lesson: a feature request is a symptom, not a spec.
What's the most-requested feature you regret building?

POST 2
My weekly planning takes 20 minutes now instead of 2 hours.
The trick: I stopped planning tasks and started planning outcomes.
Monday: write the 3 outcomes that would make this week a win.
Friday: check them honestly.
Everything else is noise.

POST 3
Hot take: most product roadmaps are just wish lists with dates.
If your roadmap doesn't say what you'll stop doing, it isn't a strategy.
