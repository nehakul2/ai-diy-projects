Podcast: "Getting unstuck" episode, ~45 min, listened on commute

- Guest argued most procrastination is about unclear next steps, not laziness
- "Two-minute rule": if the next step takes under 2 minutes, do it now
- For big tasks, write the very first physical action (e.g. "open the doc and write the heading")
- Weekly review: 20 minutes on Friday to clear inboxes and pick next week's top 3
- Recommended keeping a "parking lot" list so stray ideas don't derail focus
