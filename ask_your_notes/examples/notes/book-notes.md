# Book notes

## Atomic Habits (James Clear)
- Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement: 1% better every day.
- Make it obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying.
- "Habit stacking": after I pour my coffee, I write my 3 outcomes for the day.
- Environment beats motivation. I moved my running shoes next to the door.

## Deep Work (Cal Newport)
- Schedule deep work blocks like meetings; 90 minutes is my sweet spot.
- Shut down ritual at the end of the day: check tomorrow's calendar, say "shutdown complete".
